# Image and library provenance

`assets/meal-tray.png`: original OpenAI-generated image for this project. Prompt: Overhead editorial photograph of an open compostable takeaway tray containing vegetable pulao, golden dal, and green vegetable curry in separate compartments, warm ivory tabletop, natural warm daylight, honest simple hostel meal, landscape 3:2, no text, logos, or watermark.

Illustrative only: this is not an actual donation or GLA meal photograph. It appears in the pickup guide with a visible caption. Real listing cards do not borrow the illustration as a food photo. The 3D neighborhood is an illustrative procedural scene, not a GLA campus model.

`assets/vendor/three.module.js` and `three.core.js`: Three.js library copied from the previously installed project dependency. MIT license included at `assets/vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt`. Firebase web SDK is imported from Google's official CDN, version 12.19.0. Fonts are DM Sans and Manrope from Google Fonts.
