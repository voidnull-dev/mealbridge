# Design
Midnight forest surfaces and luminous lime accents frame an illustrative low-poly 3D food-sharing journey. A dark immersive landing section uses orbital linework, pointer response and scroll-synchronized transport animation. Operational views use pale surfaces, visible labels, high contrast, simple tables/cards and explicit approval/empty/error states. Google login precedes a one-time radio choice and verification form. Sticky desktop navigation collapses into a keyboard-operable mobile menu. Reduced-motion preferences use static scene fallbacks; WebGL and offscreen animations are lazy and paused. Illustration is not actual GLA campus imagery. No fictional metrics or organization cards.
